MandatePay

Give your AI agent a budget, not your wallet.

QDay Aevum Testnet · 44005

A mandate escrows a fixed tUSD8 budget on-chain and authorises exactly one agent address to pay exactly one payee — at most a set amount per payment, until a set expiry. The agent never holds your keys or your funds. Revoke any time and the unspent budget comes straight back. Every payment is a public, auditable event.

Owner · Create a mandate

Two transactions: approve the escrow amount, then create. The full total cap is escrowed in the contract — that is what guarantees the caps hold.

Agent · Pay within a mandate

Only the mandate's agent can pay, only to its payee, only within the caps and before expiry. The reference is hashed on-chain to bind the payment to your invoice.
